 Overwrite the ghost cells of F beyond the domain faces where F is extrapolated, by extending the planes of the interior interface. At a wall the interface then meets the wall at whatever angle it has: a free contact line. Extrapolated values impose 90 degrees instead, and the inconsistent normals near the wall unbalance gravity at a tilted interface. At an outflow the interface continues smoothly. The result does not depend on the box layout, or on the order of the faces: a ghost cell beyond several (an edge or a corner) takes the mean of their values, over those that extend a plane to it, which for a planar interface is the plane.

 Face density rho_g + (rho_l - rho_g) * <face_segment_fraction>, with the average over the given volume fractions, and each phase density a value or a field (PhaseDensity). For a planar interface normal to gravity it makes rho_face * g a discrete gradient, which is the hydrostatic balance of a balanced-force projection (Francois et al., JCP 213, 2006). Each face gets one value. On several AMR levels, call it on each: the MAC projection averages the coefficients of a fine level down onto the coarse faces it covers.

Detailed Description

Geometric volume-of-fluid transport with a piecewise-linear interface calculation (PLIC), for any AMReX-based solver.

The interface in each mixed cell is a plane, fitted to the volume fractions F (the liquid fraction of each cell) around it. The reconstruction, how the normal of the plane is computed, is selected by name: "youngs", "myc" or "myc_height". Like Godunov, MOL and BDS, the library provides spatial operators and leaves the update, the ghost cells and the synchronization of the levels to the solver.

Requires Cartesian coordinates, and has no embedded boundary support.
