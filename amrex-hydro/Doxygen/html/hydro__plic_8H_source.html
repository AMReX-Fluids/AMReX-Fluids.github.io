1/**
2 * \file hydro_plic.H
3 *
4 * Geometric volume-of-fluid transport with a piecewise-linear interface calculation (PLIC), for any
5 * AMReX-based solver.
6 *
7 * The interface in each mixed cell is a plane, fitted to the volume fractions F (the liquid fraction
8 * of each cell) around it. The reconstruction, how the normal of the plane is computed, is selected
9 * by name: "youngs", "myc" or "myc_height". Like Godunov, MOL and BDS, the library provides spatial
10 * operators and leaves the update, the ghost cells and the synchronization of the levels to the
11 * solver.
12 *
13 * Requires Cartesian coordinates, and has no embedded boundary support.
14 *
15 * \addtogroup PLIC
16 * @{
17 */
