1/**
2 * \file hydro_plic.H
3 *
4 * Geometric volume-of-fluid transport with a piecewise-linear interface calculation (PLIC), for any
5 * AMReX-based solver.
6 *
7 * The interface in each mixed cell is a plane, fitted to the volume fractions F (the liquid fraction
8 * of each cell) around it. The reconstruction, how the normal of the plane is computed, is selected
9 * by name: "youngs", "myc" or "myc_height". Like Godunov, MOL and BDS, the library provides spatial
10 * operators and leaves the update, the ghost cells and the synchronization of the levels to the
11 * solver.
12 *
13 * Requires Cartesian coordinates, and has no embedded boundary support.
14 *
15 * \addtogroup PLIC
16 * @{
17 */

62/**
63 * \brief Overwrite the ghost cells of F beyond the domain faces where F is extrapolated, by extending
64 * the planes of the interior interface. At a wall the interface then meets the wall at whatever
65 * angle it has: a free contact line. Extrapolated values impose 90 degrees instead, and the
66 * inconsistent normals near the wall unbalance gravity at a tilted interface. At an outflow the
67 * interface continues smoothly. The result does not depend on the box layout, or on the order
68 * of the faces: a ghost cell beyond several (an edge or a corner) takes the mean of their
69 * values, over those that extend a plane to it, which for a planar interface is the plane.
70 *
71 * The planes are extended beyond the wall cells within wall_nsearch cells, along the face, of the
72 * interface; beyond the others a ghost cell takes the value of its wall cell. At a shallow angle the
73 * extended plane would cross the ghost layers further from the contact line than that, where no
74 * reconstruction stencil of an interface cell reaches. The search reads no cell beyond a face whose
75 * ghost cells are kept, and a ghost cell beyond a wall and such a face keeps its value.
76 *
77 * The boundary condition of F on each non-periodic domain face selects what happens there (that of
78 * a periodic direction is not read):
79 * - foextrap, hoextrap, hoextrapcc: the ghost cells are overwritten (a wall or an outflow);
80 * - ext_dir, ext_dir_cc: the ghost cells are kept, as the volume fraction that flows in;
81 * - reflect_even: the ghost cells are kept, as the mirror image of a symmetry plane (90 degrees);
82 * - direction_dependent: the ghost cells are kept (inflow or outflow, face by face);
83 * - int_dir: nothing to do;
84 * - reflect_odd is not a valid condition for a volume fraction, and aborts.
85 *
86 * \param F volume fraction, one component, with all its ghost cells filled, including
87 * those beyond the domain (first-order extrapolation is enough at foextrap,
88 * hoextrap and hoextrapcc faces) and at coarse/fine boundaries
89 * \param geom geometry of this level
90 * \param bcrec boundary condition of F, as for its ghost cells
91 * \param reconstruction "youngs", "myc" or "myc_height"
92 */
93void FillWallGhosts (amrex::MultiFab& F, amrex::Geometry const& geom, amrex::BCRec const& bcrec,
94 std::string const& reconstruction);

96/**
97 * \brief The density of one phase for SharpFaceDensity: a value, or a cell-centered field.
98 *
99 * The field is the density of that phase, not the mixture density: it is defined in every cell,
100 * extended across the interface by the solver (a low Mach solver may give the gas density from its
101 * temperature and composition everywhere). It has the layout of the F given to SharpFaceDensity and
102 * one filled ghost layer, and a face takes the arithmetic mean of the two cells beside it. A phase
103 * density that varies along gravity is then balanced exactly away from the interface; on a face cut
104 * by the interface rho_face is off by at most |grad rho| h / 8, as the phase's part of the segment
105 * between the cell centers has its mean density at its own middle rather than at the face.
106 */
107struct PhaseDensity
108{
109 PhaseDensity (amrex::Real a_value) noexcept : value(a_value) {}
110 PhaseDensity (amrex::MultiFab const& a_field) noexcept : field(&a_field) {}
111 // It keeps a pointer to the field: not to a temporary
112 PhaseDensity (amrex::MultiFab&& a_field) = delete;
113
114 amrex::Real value = 0.0;
115 amrex::MultiFab const* field = nullptr;
116};

118/**
119 * \brief Face density rho_g + (rho_l - rho_g) * <face_segment_fraction>, with the average over the
120 * given volume fractions, and each phase density a value or a field (PhaseDensity). For a
121 * planar interface normal to gravity it makes rho_face * g a discrete gradient, which is the
122 * hydrostatic balance of a balanced-force projection (Francois et al., JCP 213, 2006). Each
123 * face gets one value. On several AMR levels, call it on each: the MAC projection averages the
124 * coefficients of a fine level down onto the coarse faces it covers.
125 *
126 * \param rho_face output, on the faces of the boxes of F with its DistributionMapping; its
127 * ghost faces, if any, are left as they are
128 * \param F the volume fractions to average, e.g. at t^n and t^{n+1} for t^{n+1/2}, with
129 * nGrowF(reconstruction) filled ghost cells
130 * \param geom geometry of this level
131 * \param rho_l liquid density, a value or a field
132 * \param rho_g gas density, a value or a field
133 * \param reconstruction "youngs", "myc" or "myc_height"
134 */
135void SharpFaceDensity (FacePtrs const& rho_face,
136 amrex::Vector<amrex::MultiFab const*> const& F,
137 amrex::Geometry const& geom,
