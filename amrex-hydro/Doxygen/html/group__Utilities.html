Enforces solvablity by scaling outflow to match with inflow.

Only boundaries with BCType::direction_dependent take part. The inflow and outflow are integrated over the tagged boundary faces, and the outflow is then scaled by influx/outflux. Each face is weighted by its own area, so 2D RZ is handled (an r-face carries 2*pi*r*dz and a z-face 2*pi*r*dr); spherical coordinates are not supported and are asserted against. The test for "no flow at all" compares the mean normal speed over the tagged faces against small_vel, so it does not depend on the length scale of the problem.

Parameters
[in,out]vels_vecPer level, the normal velocity in each direction. Each MultiFab is read and written in its boundary ghost cells, so it needs (filled) ghost cells: one in the normal direction if it is cell-centered, and one in each tangential direction if include_bndry_corners is true. This is asserted.
[in]bc_typeBoundary conditions for the normal velocity.
[in]geomPer level geometry.
[in]include_bndry_cornersAlso tag and correct the cells in the corners of the domain boundary.

Extrapolate the velocity to faces, for use as the pre-MAC velocity.

Note
Ghost-cell requirements: vel needs 3 filled ghost cells, or 4 for 3D EBGodunov; vel_forces needs 1; and velocity_on_eb_inflow, when given, needs 2.
advection_type must be one of "Godunov", "MOL" or "BDS", matching ComputeFluxesOnBoxFromState. BDS extrapolates the velocity to faces with Godunov PLM, so "BDS" here ignores godunov_ppm, and is not available with EB.
BC_MF, the position-dependent boundary conditions, is only supported with (EB)Godunov; passing it with "MOL" or "BDS" aborts, because those kernels would silently use h_bcrec/d_bcrec instead.
