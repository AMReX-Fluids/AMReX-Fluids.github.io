Detailed Description

The height-function normal along the dominant direction of the Mixed Youngs-Centered normal, or the latter where the heights are not defined (thin or strongly curved structures). Exact for a planar interface. Where components tie, the mean over the tied directions, so that no direction is preferred (the slopes are then 1 relative to each, where the heights are exact), and the MYC normal unless the heights hold along each.
