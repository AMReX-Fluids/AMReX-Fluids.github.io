Extrapolate the velocity to faces, for use as the pre-MAC velocity.

Note
Ghost-cell requirements: vel needs 4 filled ghost cells in 3D and 3 in 2D, because the 3D prediction runs on faces lo-1..hi+2 with the fourth-order slope stencil. The EB factory must carry at least as many ghost cells, vel_forces needs 1, and velocity_on_eb_inflow, when given, needs 2. These are asserted.
