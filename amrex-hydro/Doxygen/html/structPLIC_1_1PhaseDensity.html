Detailed Description

The density of one phase for SharpFaceDensity: a value, or a cell-centered field.

The field is the density of that phase, not the mixture density: it is defined in every cell, extended across the interface by the solver (a low Mach solver may give the gas density from its temperature and composition everywhere). It has the layout of the F given to SharpFaceDensity and one filled ghost layer, and a face takes the arithmetic mean of the two cells beside it. A phase density that varies along gravity is then balanced exactly away from the interface; on a face cut by the interface rho_face is off by at most |grad rho| h / 8, as the phase's part of the segment between the cell centers has its mean density at its own middle rather than at the face.
