Detailed Description

Geometric kernels of the piecewise-linear interface calculation (PLIC).

The interface in a cell is the plane m.x = alpha in unit-cube cell coordinates, with the liquid on the side m.x <= alpha (m points out of the liquid). The volume <-> plane-constant relations are the analytic ones of Scardovelli & Zaleski (JCP 164, 2000).

Derived from Kynema-SGF (formerly AMR-Wind), src/equation_systems/vof, BSD 3-Clause License, Copyright (c) 2020, Alliance for Energy Innovation and Regents of the University of California. Changes: 2D variants, per-face flux evaluation, AMReX-Hydro conventions, normal schemes as template parameters.
