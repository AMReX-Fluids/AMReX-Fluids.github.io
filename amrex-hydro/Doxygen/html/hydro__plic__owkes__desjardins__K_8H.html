 Displacement of mesh node (i,j,k) over the step, in cell units, by the midpoint rule: the velocity at the node, then the velocity half a step back along it. Velocities between faces are multilinear interpolations of the face velocities themselves, so that a node of a box reads no faces beyond the first ghost layer, and the arithmetic is relative to the node, so that every box holding it, and its periodic images, get the same displacement.
