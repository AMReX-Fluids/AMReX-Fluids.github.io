AMREX_GPU_HOST_DEVICE AMREX_FORCE_INLINE bool height_normal (const int i, const int j, const int k, const int d, amrex::Array4< amrex::Real const > const &F, amrex::Real m[3])
 Height-function normal with height direction d: the liquid heights of the 7-cell columns through the tangential neighbors of (i,j,k), differenced centrally. The columns are centered on the row where the column through (i,j,k) crosses the interface, at most one row from it, so they read up to hf_half + 1 cells along d. Exact for a planar interface whose slopes relative to d are at most 1, which holds for the dominant normal direction: the crossing of a neighboring column then lies within 2.5 cells of that row, and its end cells are pure. Returns false unless each column, the center one included, crosses the interface once: from pure liquid to pure gas in the orientation of the center column, with F monotone in between. A thin gas gap with a liquid sliver inside a column is not a height, and the caller falls back to another normal.

◆ FillWallGhosts()

void PLIC::FillWallGhosts ( amrex::MultiFab & F,
amrex::Geometry const & geom,
amrex::BCRec const & bcrec,
std::string const & reconstruction )

Overwrite the ghost cells of F beyond the domain faces where F is extrapolated, by extending the planes of the interior interface. At a wall the interface then meets the wall at whatever angle it has: a free contact line. Extrapolated values impose 90 degrees instead, and the inconsistent normals near the wall unbalance gravity at a tilted interface. At an outflow the interface continues smoothly. The result does not depend on the box layout, or on the order of the faces: a ghost cell beyond several (an edge or a corner) takes the mean of their values, over those that extend a plane to it, which for a planar interface is the plane.

The planes are extended beyond the wall cells within wall_nsearch cells, along the face, of the interface; beyond the others a ghost cell takes the value of its wall cell. At a shallow angle the extended plane would cross the ghost layers further from the contact line than that, where no reconstruction stencil of an interface cell reaches. The search reads no cell beyond a face whose ghost cells are kept, and a ghost cell beyond a wall and such a face keeps its value.

The boundary condition of F on each non-periodic domain face selects what happens there (that of a periodic direction is not read):

  • foextrap, hoextrap, hoextrapcc: the ghost cells are overwritten (a wall or an outflow);
  • ext_dir, ext_dir_cc: the ghost cells are kept, as the volume fraction that flows in;
  • reflect_even: the ghost cells are kept, as the mirror image of a symmetry plane (90 degrees);
  • direction_dependent: the ghost cells are kept (inflow or outflow, face by face);
  • int_dir: nothing to do;
  • reflect_odd is not a valid condition for a volume fraction, and aborts.
Parameters

geomgeometry of this level
bcrecboundary condition of F, as for its ghost cells
reconstruction"youngs", "myc" or "myc_height"

Height-function normal with height direction d: the liquid heights of the 7-cell columns through the tangential neighbors of (i,j,k), differenced centrally. The columns are centered on the row where the column through (i,j,k) crosses the interface, at most one row from it, so they read up to hf_half + 1 cells along d. Exact for a planar interface whose slopes relative to d are at most 1, which holds for the dominant normal direction: the crossing of a neighboring column then lies within 2.5 cells of that row, and its end cells are pure. Returns false unless each column, the center one included, crosses the interface once: from pure liquid to pure gas in the orientation of the center column, with F monotone in between. A thin gas gap with a liquid sliver inside a column is not a height, and the caller falls back to another normal.

template<typename Normal>

const int dir,

const int sgn,
amrex::Array4< int const > const & cand,
amrex::Array4< amrex::Real const > const & pl,
amrex::Real * m,

The PLIC planes that the ghost cells beyond the wall cell iv_w extend, when the interface meets the wall within wall_nsearch cells (tangentially) of iv_w: a free contact line, exact for a planar interface and for one uniform normal to the wall. The planes are those of the nearest interface cells, up to Normal::stencil layers inside the wall, whose reconstruction does not read ghost cells (Normal::reach). Below about 26.6 degrees from the wall, or near another wall, where myc_height finds none, the MYC planes of the nearest interface cells below the wall layer, which are exact there (see wall_nsearch), down to about atan(1/wall_nsearch). Below that, the interface runs inside the wall layer near the contact line, and the planes are those of the nearest wall cells where the one-sided height function of wall_layer_normal holds. All the equally near cells give their plane, so that no direction is preferred. No plane reads ghost cells, so one pass of the fill is enough.
