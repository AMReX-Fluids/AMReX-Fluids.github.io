struct  HeightFunctionMYC
 The height-function normal along the dominant direction of the Mixed Youngs-Centered normal, or the latter where the heights are not defined (thin or strongly curved structures). Exact for a planar interface. Where components tie, the mean over the tied directions, so that no direction is preferred (the slopes are then 1 relative to each, where the heights are exact), and the MYC normal unless the heights hold along each. AMREX_GPU_HOST_DEVICE AMREX_FORCE_INLINE bool height_normal (const int i, const int j, const int k, const int d, amrex::Array4< amrex::Real const > const &F, amrex::Real m[3])
 Height-function normal with height direction d: the liquid heights of the 7-cell columns through the tangential neighbors of (i,j,k), differenced centrally. The columns are centered on the row where the column through (i,j,k) crosses the interface, at most one row from it, so they read up to hf_half + 1 cells along d. Exact for a planar interface whose slopes relative to d are at most 1, which holds for the dominant normal direction: the crossing of a neighboring column then lies within 2.5 cells of that row, and its end cells are pure. Returns false unless each column, the center one included, crosses the interface once: from pure liquid to pure gas in the orientation of the center column, with F monotone in between. A thin gas gap with a liquid sliver inside a column is not a height, and the caller falls back to another normal.

 Whether cell (i,j,k) lies on a structure one cell thick (a film, a gap, a filament or a drop), where the gradient is noise and the normal comes from the Hessian instead (degenerate_normal): along some line through it (an axis or a diagonal) its neighbors on both sides are pure in the same phase, and it differs from them by more than 8 times as much as F changes across it along any line.
